About me

I am a postdoctoral research engineer at CEA List in the LSEA Laboratory, Paris-Saclay University. My work sits at the intersection of ontology engineering and sustainability: I design and maintain formal knowledge representations that make industrial and environmental data interoperable, traceable, and regulation-ready.

My main focus is on Digital Product Passports (DPP) and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) ontologies for the circular economy. This involves modelling complex material, component, and environmental impact data in OWL 2 DL, writing SPARQL queries and SHACL constraint sets for validation, and aligning heterogeneous ontologies across industrial partners. I work closely with European regulatory frameworks — ESPR and JTC-24 standardisation — and contribute to several EU and ANR projects.

I completed my PhD in 2023 at Université Grenoble Alpes, with a thesis on foundational ontological relations theory (FORT), preceded by an M2 internship at INRIA Grenoble.

I work bilingually in French and English and collaborate regularly with academic and industrial partners across Europe.
