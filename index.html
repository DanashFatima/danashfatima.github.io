About me

I build formal knowledge representations for sustainability — digital product passports, life cycle assessment, circular economy.

Since November 2023 I am a postdoctoral research engineer at CEA List in the LSEA Laboratory, Paris-Saclay University. My work sits at the intersection of ontology engineering and sustainability: I design and maintain formal ontologies on subject domains related to Digital Product Passports (DPP) and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) ontologies for the circular economy. This involves modelling complex data in OWL 2 DL, writing SPARQL queries and SHACL constraint sets for validation, aligning heterogeneous ontologies across industrial partners, and working closely with European regulatory frameworks — ESPR and JTC-24 standardisation — in the context of EU and ANR projects.

I completed my PhD in September 2023 within the STeamer team in the Laboratoire d'Informatique de Grenoble (LIG) at Université Grenoble Alpes, with a thesis on foundational ontological relations theory (FORT).

Before that, I obtained my M2 in internship at INRIA Grenoble.

I work bilingually in French and English and collaborate regularly with academic and industrial partners across Europe.
